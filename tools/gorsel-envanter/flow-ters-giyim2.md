# Flow turu 32: Ters giyim, ikinci deneme (İngilizce)

Tur 31'den alınanlar: pantolon (düz / içi dışına), sivri/küt yaprak.
Flow ayakkabıyı ters çeviremiyor. Bu yüzden ayakkabı ve terliklerde Flow'dan yalnızca DÜZ halini istiyoruz; ters halini Claude kendisi yapacak (her teki yerinde aynalayıp burnunu dışa çevirerek). Bunun için arka plan DÜZ ve TEK RENK olmalı, tahta zemin olmamalı.
Kişili görsellerde (şapka, kazak, tişört) Flow'da ilk görseli üretip AYNI görsel üzerinde "düzenle" ile eşini iste. Önceki turda "Modify" ile yapılanlar aynı çocuğu korudu.

## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). We are teaching "wearing clothes the RIGHT way vs the WRONG way (backwards / inside out)".

RULES:
1. Photorealistic, bright, soft even light, NO shadows.
2. No text, no logos, no letters anywhere, including clothing labels (labels must be blank).
3. No white clothing.
4. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
5. For pairs marked "EDIT", make the second image by editing the first one: same child, same face, same pose, same background, same camera. Change ONLY what is described.
6. Stop after every 4 images and wait for my approval.

IMAGES:
1. Top-down view, looking straight down: a pair of red children's sneakers with white laces, side by side, toes pointing up in the frame, worn on the correct feet layout (left shoe on the left, right shoe on the right), small gap between them. Plain solid pure white background, no floor texture, no shadow.
2. Top-down view, looking straight down: a pair of yellow children's slippers, side by side, toes pointing up in the frame, left on the left, right on the right, small gap between them. Plain solid pure white background, no shadow.
3. Top-down view, looking straight down: a pair of blue children's rain boots, side by side, toes pointing up in the frame, left on the left, right on the right, small gap between them. Plain solid pure white background, no shadow.
4. A blue children's t-shirt with a big yellow star on the front, laid flat, front side up. Plain solid pure white background.
5. EDIT of image 4: the SAME t-shirt turned INSIDE OUT: thick seams visible along the sides and shoulders, a blank label showing at the neck, the yellow star seen faintly from the back side of the fabric. Same white background, same position.
6. A smiling 5-year-old child standing in front of a plain light-beige wall, wearing a red baseball cap the normal way, the brim pointing forward over the face. Front view, waist up.
7. EDIT of image 6: the SAME child wears the SAME cap BACKWARDS, the brim pointing behind the head. Nothing else changes.
8. A smiling 5-year-old child standing in front of a plain light-beige wall, wearing a dark green sweater the normal way. Front view, waist up.
9. EDIT of image 8: the SAME child wears the SAME sweater INSIDE OUT: thick seams clearly visible on the outside along the shoulders, sides and sleeves, and a blank label sticking out at the front of the neck. Nothing else changes.
10. A smiling 5-year-old child standing in front of a plain light-beige wall, wearing a blue t-shirt with a big yellow star on the front, worn the normal way. Front view, waist up.
11. EDIT of image 10: the SAME child wears the SAME t-shirt BACK TO FRONT: the star is NOT visible (it is on the back now), the plain back of the t-shirt is in front, and the higher back collar with a blank label is under the chin. Nothing else changes.

Start with IMAGE 1.
