## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, intellectual disability, Down syndrome). We are teaching "clothes worn the RIGHT way vs INSIDE OUT / BACK TO FRONT".

RULES:
1. Photorealistic, bright, soft even light, plain solid pure white background, no shadow. Square image, the item centered and large.
2. No text, no logos, no letters anywhere; clothing labels must be blank.
3. No white clothing.
4. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
5. For images marked "EDIT", make the image by editing the previous one: same item, same colour, same size, same position, same camera. Change ONLY what is described. The difference must be VERY clear.
6. Stop after every 4 images and wait for my approval.

INSIDE OUT (clothes laid flat, top-down view):
1. A dark green children's sweater, laid flat, front side up, sleeves spread.
2. EDIT of 1: the SAME sweater turned INSIDE OUT: thick seams clearly visible along the sides, shoulders and sleeves, a blank label showing at the neck, the fabric looks rougher.
3. A pair of red children's socks, laid flat side by side.
4. EDIT of 3: the SAME socks turned INSIDE OUT: rough inner texture, loose thread ends and seams visible at the toes and heels.
5. Orange children's shorts, laid flat, front side up.
6. EDIT of 5: the SAME shorts turned INSIDE OUT: white pocket linings hanging out on both sides, seams visible.
7. A purple children's hoodie, laid flat, front side up, hood above the collar.
8. EDIT of 7: the SAME hoodie turned INSIDE OUT: seams visible on sleeves and sides, a blank label at the neck, the inside of the hood showing.
9. A yellow children's dress with small blue flowers, laid flat, front side up.
10. EDIT of 9: the SAME dress turned INSIDE OUT: the flowers only faintly visible through the fabric, seams and hem stitching clearly visible on the outside.
11. Blue children's jogging pants, laid flat, front side up.
12. EDIT of 11: the SAME jogging pants turned INSIDE OUT: pocket linings sticking out, seams along the legs, a blank label at the waist.

BACK TO FRONT (the same brown teddy bear sitting, front view, facing the camera):
13. A brown teddy bear sitting, front view, wearing a red baseball cap the normal way, the brim pointing forward.
14. EDIT of 13: the SAME bear wears the SAME cap BACKWARDS, the brim pointing behind its head. Nothing else changes.
15. The SAME brown teddy bear sitting, front view, wearing a blue t-shirt with a big yellow star on the front, worn the normal way.
16. EDIT of 15: the SAME bear wears the SAME t-shirt BACK TO FRONT: the star is NOT visible (it is on its back), the plain back of the t-shirt is in front, the higher back collar with a blank label is under its chin. Nothing else changes.
17. The SAME brown teddy bear sitting, front view, wearing a green hoodie the normal way, the hood lying behind its head.
18. EDIT of 17: the SAME bear wears the SAME hoodie BACK TO FRONT: the hood hangs down on its chest in front, the face is fully visible. Nothing else changes.
19. The SAME brown teddy bear sitting, front view, wearing an orange apron with a big front pocket, worn the normal way on its belly.
20. EDIT of 19: the SAME bear wears the SAME apron on its BACK: its belly is uncovered, only the apron ties are visible in front, the apron hangs behind. Nothing else changes.
