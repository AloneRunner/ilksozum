## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). Three topics: DAY vs NIGHT, FAST vs SLOW, and one missing picture for BEHIND.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic photos, simple and clear, nothing scary.
2. No text, no logos, no letters, no signs anywhere.
3. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
4. For pairs, make the second image by using the first one as reference: SAME place, SAME objects, SAME camera angle. Change ONLY what is described.
5. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.

PART A: ONE IMAGE FOR "BEHIND"
1. Plain light-grey studio background. A white wire birdcage stands on the floor, facing the camera, door closed. A small green-yellow budgie stands on the floor BEHIND the cage, clearly seen through the bars, smaller because it is further back. Nothing is in front of the cage.

PART B: DAY vs NIGHT (same place, same camera; DAY = bright sunlight, blue sky; NIGHT = dark blue sky, moon and stars, lights on. The night picture must still be clearly visible, not pitch black.)
