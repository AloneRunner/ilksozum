## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-8, autism).

RULES:
1. Soft, friendly cartoon style, like a modern children's animated film or picture book. Bright warm colors, simple backgrounds. NOT photorealistic. No text, no letters, no logos, no numbers.
2. Calm, happy scenes. Nothing scary, no tears, no needles, no injuries.
3. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never edit a previous one.
4. In one group, the SAME characters in every picture: same face, same hair, same clothes, same place.
5. Square images (1:1).
6. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.

GROUP 1 — Visiting the doctor. A cartoon girl (5 years old, two short black pigtails, yellow dress), her mother (blue dress), a smiling female doctor with glasses in a white coat. A bright, friendly clinic.
1. The girl and her mother sit in a colorful waiting room; the girl looks at a picture book.
2. The doctor opens her office door and waves hello; the girl waves back.
3. The girl sits on her mother's lap next to the doctor's desk; a teddy bear is on the desk.
4. The doctor listens to the teddy bear's tummy with a stethoscope; the girl watches and smiles.
5. The doctor gives the girl a colorful star sticker; the girl smiles.

GROUP 2 — At the supermarket. A cartoon boy (5 years old, short brown hair, green sweater, blue trousers), his father (short beard, grey jacket). A calm supermarket.
6. The boy and his father walk into the supermarket; the father pushes a shopping cart.
7. The boy puts a red apple into the shopping cart.
8. The boy points at cookies on a shelf; his father gently shakes his head and smiles.
9. The boy and his father wait in line at the checkout.
10. The boy carries a small shopping bag out of the store, smiling.

GROUP 3 — Brushing teeth. The same cartoon boy as Group 2 in a blue t-shirt, at a bathroom sink with a mirror.

13. The boy brushes his teeth.
14. The boy rinses his mouth with water from a cup.
15. The boy smiles in the mirror with clean teeth.

GROUP 4 — Getting ready to go outside. The same cartoon girl as Group 1, wearing a pink sweater and blue trousers, at the front door of a house.
