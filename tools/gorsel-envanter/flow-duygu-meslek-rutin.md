# Flow turu 35 ve 36: Duygular, Duyularımız, Meslekler (Bu Kimin?), günlük rutin, odalar ve kaplar (İngilizce)

Kaan: "Mini oyun görsellerini kavram etkinliklerinin görselleriyle birlikte yaparız. Duygular etkinliği zaten var."

Nereye gidecek:
- **Duygular:** kavram etkinliği. Çift: aynı çocuk, iki duygu. "Hangi çocuk mutlu?"
- **Duyularımız:** göz, kulak, burun, dil, el. Eşleşecek nesneler (çiçek, davul, elma...) nesne turunda zaten gelecek.
- **Meslekler:** Bu Kimin? etkinliğindeki sahipler (önlük → aşçı, itfaiye aracı → itfaiyeci...).
- **Odalar:** Nereye Ait? mini oyunu. Eşyalar (kaşık, diş fırçası...) nesne görsellerinden kullanılacak.
- **Kaplar:** Oda Temizliği mini oyunu.
- **Günlük rutin (tur 36):** Sıralı Ol! mini oyunu. Önce/Sonra kavramında da kullanılabilir.

Flow gerekmeyenler:
- **Tek/Çift ve Kaç Tane:** Claude, nesne görsellerini çoğaltarak yapacak (3 elma, 4 elma...).
- **Sesi Bul:** ElevenLabs sesleri gelince.

Kişili çiftlerde 2 deneme kuralı geçerli: aynı çocuk iki denemede de gelmezse ikinci denemenin görselleri alınır.

## TUR 35: ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). Topics: EMOTIONS, OUR SENSES, JOBS, ROOMS and STORAGE BOXES.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic photos, bright and soft light, simple. NOT cartoon.
2. No text, no logos, no letters anywhere.
3. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
4. For an emotion set, all images of a set must show the SAME child, same clothes, same plain background, same camera; ONLY the facial expression changes. Make expressions very clear and easy to read, like a picture card.
5. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.

PART A: EMOTIONS (head and shoulders, plain light background, child looks at the camera)
Set 1: a 5-year-old boy with short brown hair in a yellow t-shirt.
1. HAPPY: big smile, eyes bright.

## TUR 36: ajana yapıştırılacak metin (günlük rutin)

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6). Topic: DAILY ROUTINE, steps of the day.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic photos, bright, simple, NOT cartoon. No text or logos.
2. All images show the SAME 5-year-old child (short brown hair), so the child is recognizable in every picture. Home scenes are simple and tidy.
3. Each image shows ONE clear action. The action must be easy to understand at a glance.
4. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.

IMAGES:
1. Waking up: the child sits up in bed, stretching arms, morning light.
2. Making the bed: the child pulls the blanket straight on the bed.
3. Washing the face at the bathroom sink.
4. Getting dressed: the child pulls a t-shirt over the head.
5. Eating breakfast at the kitchen table.
6. Brushing teeth at the sink with a toothbrush.
7. Putting on shoes, sitting on a small bench by the door.
8. Leaving for school with a backpack, waving at the door.
9. Washing hands with soap at the sink, lots of foam.
10. Eating lunch with a spoon at the table.
11. Taking a bath in a bathtub with bubbles.
12. Putting on pajamas.
13. Reading a picture book in bed with a small lamp on.
14. Sleeping in bed, eyes closed, teddy bear next to the pillow.

Start with IMAGE 1.
