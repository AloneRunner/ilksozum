patates kızartması, omlet, şeker). Yoğurt listede iki kez var; bir tane çekilip ikisine bağlanacak.

## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). This is a set of single-food cards to learn the names of foods.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic studio photo, bright soft light, NOT cartoon, NOT illustration.
2. ONE item in the middle of a plain pure white background, filling about 65% of the picture. Show the most typical, easy-to-recognize Turkish home version of the food.
3. Dishes are served on a COLORED plate or in a COLORED bowl (not white), so they do not disappear on the white background.
4. No text, no logos, no labels, no brand names, no hands, no people.
5. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
6. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.
