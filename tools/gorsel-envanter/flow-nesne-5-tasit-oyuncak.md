## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). This is a set of single-object cards to learn the names of things.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic studio photo, bright soft light, NOT cartoon, NOT illustration.
2. ONE object, the whole object visible, side or three-quarter view, in the middle of a plain pure white background, filling about 65% of the picture. Real vehicles are shown as real full-size vehicles (not toys), photographed on plain white.
3. If the object is mostly white, use a plain light grey background instead.
4. No text, no logos, no brand names, no numbers on vehicles, no people (unless the item says so).
5. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
6. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.
