## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). This is a set of cards to learn the names of people, jobs and everyday objects.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic photos, bright soft light, NOT cartoon, NOT illustration.
2. PEOPLE: one person, waist up, facing the camera, friendly smile, plain light background; the job is clear from the clothes or a typical object. Adults are real adults.
3. OBJECTS: one object, whole object visible, in the middle of a plain pure white background, filling about 65% of the picture. The object is NOT white; if it is usually white, use a plain light grey background.
4. No text, no logos, no brand names, no numbers or letters on objects.
5. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
6. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.
