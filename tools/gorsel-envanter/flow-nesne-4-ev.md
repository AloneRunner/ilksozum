## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). This is a set of single-object cards to learn the names of things at home.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic studio photo, bright soft light, NOT cartoon, NOT illustration.
2. ONE object, the whole object visible, in the middle of a plain pure white background, filling about 65% of the picture. Show the most common modern version of the object found in a Turkish home.
3. The object itself must NOT be white (white disappears on the white background). If the real object is usually white (toilet, sink, bathtub, washing machine, fridge, toilet paper, cotton...), use a plain light grey background instead.
4. Screens are turned off or show a plain blue color. No text, no logos, no brand names, no people.
5. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
6. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.
