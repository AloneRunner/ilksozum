## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). This is a set of animal cards to learn animal names.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic photo of a REAL animal (not a toy, not cartoon), bright soft light.
2. ONE animal, the WHOLE body visible, side or three-quarter view, standing or sitting in its typical pose, in the middle of a plain pure white background, filling about 65% of the picture.
3. If the animal is white or very light (swan, polar animals, sheep), use a plain light grey background instead.
4. Friendly, calm look. Nothing scary: no open jaws with teeth, no blood, no fighting.
5. No text, no logos, no people.
6. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
7. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.
