## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). This is a set of cards to learn the names of plants, nature, clothes and body parts.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic photos, bright and clear, NOT cartoon, NOT illustration.
2. OBJECTS (plants, clothes): one object, whole object visible, in the middle of a plain pure white background, filling about 65% of the picture. The object is NOT white; if it is usually white, use a plain light grey background.
3. NATURE AND PLACES: a simple, clear landscape photo where the named thing is the main subject, in the middle, easy to recognize.
4. BODY PARTS: a close-up of a child's (about 6 years old) body part on a plain light background; friendly, clean, natural.
5. No text, no logos, no brand names.
6. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
7. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.
