## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). This is a set of single-object cards to learn the names of foods.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic studio photo, bright soft light, NOT cartoon, NOT illustration.
2. ONE object in the middle of a plain pure white background, the object fills about 65% of the picture. Show the most typical, easy-to-recognize form of the food (whole, not cut, unless the item says otherwise).
3. If the food itself is white or very light (milk, ayran, rice, yogurt), put it in a COLORED cup or bowl so it does not disappear on the white background.
4. No text, no logos, no labels, no brand names, no hands, no people.
5. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
6. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.
