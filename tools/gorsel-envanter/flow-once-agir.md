## Ajana yapıştırılacak metin

I am making picture cards for young children in special education (ages 3-6: autism, language delay). Topics: BEFORE vs AFTER, HEAVY vs LIGHT, and one FAST picture.

RULES:
1. Photorealistic photos, bright and simple. NOT cartoon, no faces or eyes on objects.
2. No text, no logos, no letters anywhere.
3. No white objects.
4. Create every image as a SEPARATE NEW image. Never overwrite a previous one.
5. For pairs, make the second image by using the first one as reference: SAME object, SAME place, SAME camera angle. Change ONLY what is described.
6. Stop after every 5 images and wait for my approval.

PART A: FAST
1. A real red race car speeding on a race track, photographed from the side, motion blur in the background. Realistic, no face, no eyes.

PART B: BEFORE vs AFTER (same object, two moments in time; the change must be very clear)
2. BEFORE: a whole red apple on a wooden table.
3. AFTER: the same apple on the same table, half eaten with big bite marks, the core showing.
